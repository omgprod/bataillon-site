Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?

Yes. All six campaign missions and the daily challenge are free to play. Upgrades use coins earned in runs; no real-money payments are currently enabled.

Do I need an account?

No. Start playing without signing up. Records, coins, stars and preferences are saved locally on your device.

Are there ads?

No. Bataillon shows no ads and includes no advertising tracking.

Where are my records and progress stored?

Stars, coins, outfits and records are kept locally on your device. Deleting the app or resetting your device may erase this save.

Can I play the daily challenge offline?

Yes. The daily route and your personal best are available offline. Game Center leaderboards are not enabled in the current version.

Can I recover a deleted save?

The current version does not sync saves to an account. If local data is deleted, it generally cannot be restored.

Does the shop charge real money?

No. In-app purchases and real-money payments are not enabled in the current version.

Which devices are supported?

Bataillon is designed for iPhone and iPad. Availability depends on the version released on the App Store.
