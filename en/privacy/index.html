Data & transparency

Privacy
policy.

Updated: 25 October 2026 · Applies to the current mobile game Bataillon.

◉
The game does not collect personal data.

No account, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

1. Publisher

Bataillon is published by Babapp. For questions about this policy or your data, email contact@babapp.fr.

2. Data processed by the game

The game does not require an account or send data to a server operated by its publisher. It currently includes no advertising, audience measurement or advertising tracking tools.

The game stores a local file on your device to save progress. It may include records, stars, coins, upgrades, outfits and game preferences. This information stays on your device and is not sent to the publisher.

3. Apple services

In the version described here, Game Center leaderboards are not enabled and Bataillon sends no player data to Game Center. iOS and the App Store may process information according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. Those activities are governed by Apple, not this policy.

If a future version enables Game Center, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

4. Freemium and payments

Bataillon is free to play: all six campaign missions and the daily challenge are available without purchase. Upgrades are earned with coins won in the game.

No in-app purchases or real-money payments are enabled in the current version. If this changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

5. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. The publisher does not keep a copy. Uninstalling may make your progress impossible to recover.

6. Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other players. It includes no chat or player-published content.

7. Changes

This page will be updated if the game’s features or data practices change. The date above shows the current version.

8. Questions and requests

For privacy requests, email contact@babapp.fr. Do not include personal information in a public GitHub issue. Since the game does not send personal data to the publisher, it has no player file to access or delete.

About this website

This presentation website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may process technical connection data to provide and secure the site under its own privacy policy. The Bataillon website does not add advertising or analytics tracking.
